# Terms of Service

*Last updated: 26 September 2026*

These terms describe how BookEase may be used. BookEase is an academic
project built for an Advanced Programming assignment. It is a working
demonstration system, not a commercial service, and these terms are
written for that context rather than to claim compliance with any
specific consumer-protection or data-protection law.

## 1. Accounts and roles

- Every account is one of three roles: **customer**, **provider**, or
  **administrator**. Customers can later convert their own account to a
  provider account from their profile settings; providers and
  administrators are not able to switch roles through the interface.
- You are responsible for the accuracy of the information you register
  with and for keeping your password and any enabled two-factor
  credentials confidential.
- Accounts can be suspended by an administrator, for example for misuse
  of the platform or for demonstration/testing purposes. A suspended
  account cannot sign in or use the API until it is reactivated.

## 2. Bookings and cancellations

- Customers may reserve any active, future, non-full availability slot
  belonging to an active service from an active business.
- A booking starts in the **pending** state and must be **confirmed** or
  **rejected** by the provider. Confirmed bookings can later be marked
  **completed** or **cancelled**.
- Either the customer or the provider can cancel an eligible pending or
  confirmed booking; a reason may be recorded with the cancellation.
- Every status change is recorded in the booking's status history and is
  visible to both the customer and the provider involved.

## 3. Reviews

- A customer may leave one rating and comment per completed booking,
  including for bookings made before the account became a provider
  account.
- Reviews should reflect a genuine appointment experience. Reviews may be
  removed by an administrator if they are found to be abusive, unrelated
  to the booking, or otherwise misused.

## 4. Provider responsibilities

- Providers are responsible for the accuracy of their business profile,
  service descriptions, pricing and published availability.
- A new business starts in a **pending** state and becomes visible to
  customers only once an administrator approves it. Administrators may
  suspend a business or its owning account at any time.

## 5. Acceptable use

- Do not attempt to access another account, another business's data, or
  administrative functions you have not been granted.
- Do not use the platform to submit fraudulent bookings, fake reviews, or
  false business information.
- Automated or excessive use of the API beyond the documented endpoints
  and rate limits is not permitted.

## 6. Demonstration data

- This deployment may contain seeded demonstration accounts, businesses
  and bookings created for evaluation and presentation purposes. That
  data is not real and should not be relied upon.

## 7. Changes and contact

BookEase is an evolving student project; these terms may change as the
system changes. Questions about these terms can be directed to the
project maintainer listed in the project's repository/documentation.
